About The Routine Parent

The Routine Parent is written from the perspective of a hands-on parent raising two children, a daughter now 9yrs and a son 6yrs.

This blog documents the ongoing process of balancing parenting, personal development, and everyday responsibilities.

Rather than presenting perfect solutions, the blog focuses on real experiences, lessons learned, and practical systems that help manage family life while continuing to grow as a person.

The Routine Parent is about growing alongside your children while building a meaningful and balanced life.
